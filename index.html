Neural Architecture • SentenceTransformer Bi-Encoder

1. Dense Embedding Vector Space (384-d) Queries and synopses are mapped into dense latent vectors using all-MiniLM-L6-v2. This captures conceptual semantics regardless of exact keyword overlap.
2. Affective Valence Inversion When negative emotion or sadness is detected in user input, Filmo calculates inverse valence vectors to curate heartwarming, inspirational cinema with positive sentiment coefficients (+0.70 to +0.95).
3. Cosine Similarity Re-Ranking Recommendations are sorted by multi-layer cosine inner products combined with IMDb rating priors.
